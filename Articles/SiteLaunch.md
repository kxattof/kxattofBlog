Hey there! I'm Kxattof, and I created this website as a platform to pursue my passion for investigative journalism. As of now, I am the only one publishing here, with the occasional guest writer but, I hope to expand my team in the future.

## About Me:

This section is essentially a repeat of my <a> /about;;about page</a>.

Kxattof is all about providing information that has not yet been uncovered. Often times, my biggest frustration while researching is that sources mirror one another and not providing any new information. I believe very strongly in the freedom of information and think that it's extremely valuable to have well-researched, transparent publications. For these reasons, I've made it my mission to only publish valuable information and draw new insights, not echo what is already out there.

I plan on writing about a variety of topics that I think are relevant and important. I value human-made work, so I will not be using AI in the researching or writing process in any of my articles. In my mission to provide well-researched and transparent information, I also think that journalism should be uninfluenced by investors or sponsors. Although I eventually hope to have my work sponsored, I will never let money influence the way that Kxattof operates. I am also heavily against data collection and targeted advertising, so I will not be hosting ads on my website. This also means that I will not collect or sell your data. You can read more about that in our <a> /privacy;;privacy policy</a>.

## The Future of Kxattof:

I touched on this a bit, but as of now, Kxattof is a one-person operation. I do have high hopes that Kxattof will expand eventually, and I'm excited to give a platform for other small journalists. For now though, I'm the only one posting to the site.

At the time of publishing this article, the website is still a work in progress. I don't have any specific plans for the long-term plans of Kxattof other than to continue to provide high-effort publications however, if Kxattof gains traction, I will be taking that opporotunity to expand. For now though, it's just me and my humble website.
