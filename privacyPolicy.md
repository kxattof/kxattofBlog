At Kxattof, I'm a strong believer in the protection of privacy. For that reason, we at Kxattof do not collect, share, or sell any of your data beyond what we clearly and directly disclose. 

We keep information that you give us directly through emails and contact forms in our records. We keep record of names/nicknames, emails, and the content of what you wrote to us. This way we can refer to any information given to us and reply back directly.

We never share any of the information we collect with anybody outside Kxattof unless given direct, explicit consent. 

If you want us to erase any of your information from our records, please reach out to us and we'd be more than happy to!

Tl;dr: We keep track of who contacts us, what they said, and why. That's it.

We reserve the right to change this policy at any time without prior notification. We will make attempts to notify users when it has changed.
